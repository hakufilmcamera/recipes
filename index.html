Why it looks different

We take the iPhone
look out first.

Every iPhone photo goes through Apple's tone curve, which is why they all look alike. Haku keeps Apple's noise reduction and detail, then undoes the curve, turns the picture into the log signal film looks are built for, and grades it there. Shoot ProRAW, and Apple's curve is never applied at all.

Film looks

Ten looks.
And any you import.

Each look is authored from measured colour science, and Haku converts the picture into a log film space before it is applied — so it lands like film, not like a preset.

One photograph, ten looks — rendered through the same colour pipeline as the app

Who makes Haku

A small team,
and everyone who shoots with it.

Haku is made in Canada by a small but passionate development team. There is no support desk and no investor's roadmap: when you write, the people who make the app read it, and what you ask for shapes what we build into Haku next.

We read everything

In Haku, Settings › About has Report an issue and Suggest a feature. Both come straight to us, with your build and device filled in, so tell us what broke or what you would love it to do.

Bugs fixed fast

A bug you report goes to the front of the line. Fixes ship in the next update, often within days, and What's New in the app says exactly what changed.

Always adding

Haku keeps growing, with new tools and improvements arriving in regular updates. Ask for something, and what people ask for decides what we build next.

Built with you

Recipes in the community come from the people who use Haku, and your votes decide which rise to the top.
