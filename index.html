haku

A film camera, not a filter

Haku is a camera app for iPhone. Real manual dials, film simulations applied through a proper colour pipeline, and editing that never touches your original.

Join the beta Free on TestFlight while Haku is in beta.

The looks

Nine film looks authored from measured curves ship with the app, and the importer opens the film simulation LUTs Fujifilm publishes free — or any other F‑Log2 cube.

Real dials

ISO, shutter, focus, white balance and exposure compensation, each with a manual override — and every control explains itself when you hold it.

Nothing is baked in

Every photograph is the original plus a recipe. Change the look, the crop or the grain months later — the negative is always underneath.

Beyond one press

Long exposures, multiple exposure with CLONE, 48 MP ProRAW, contact sheets and carousels — a darkroom in the camera.

Support

Something wrong, or something missing? Email support@hakufilmcamera.app. If the app crashed, Settings › About in Haku fills in the build and device for you, which is most of what a fix needs.

Privacy

Haku has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and your photos never leave your phone unless you send them somewhere yourself. The full policy says exactly what the app does and does not do.
