Report a bug or suggest a feature

The quickest way is from inside Haku, because it fills in your build and device for us:

  1. Open Settings in Haku.
  2. Go to About.
  3. Tap Report an issue or Suggest a feature, and tell us what happened or what you would love it to do. A screenshot or screen recording helps.

Common questions

Where are my photos?

In Haku's library: tap the thumbnail beside the shutter. Whether they also go to Apple Photos depends on Settings › Library › New photos - see Where photos are saved.

Can I change the look after taking a photo?

Yes, always. The original is kept and the look is a recipe beside it: open the photo, choose another look or strength on the Film tab, or hold the photo to see the original.

I deleted the app. Are my photos gone?

Photos kept only in Haku are deleted with the app. Photos saved to Apple Photos stay there. Before deleting Haku, export anything you want to keep.

What is the Darkroom?

The one-time unlock for the finishing tools - curves, HSL, retouching, the print stage, long and multiple exposure, carousels, contact sheets, importing looks, editing a whole roll at once and more. The camera, all ten looks and the core editor are free. Photos you have already edited keep rendering exactly as they are.

How do I restore my Darkroom purchase?

Open any Darkroom tool to bring up the Darkroom, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Haku record where photos were taken?

Only if you turn on Geotag Photos (Settings › Library) and allow location. The place goes into the photo itself and nowhere else.

Does Haku collect any data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.

Recipes in the community

Every recipe is checked before it is published. If one is offensive, misnamed, or copies someone else's work, open it on the recipes page and use Report this recipe, or email support@hakufilmcamera.app with its name.
